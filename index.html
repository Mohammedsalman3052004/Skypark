ABOUT SOHAN REAALTY

Sohan Reaalty (Sohan Developers) is a Bangalore-based real estate developer with over a decade of experience in creating premium homes that blend quality, trust, and thoughtful design. From our earliest projects to our newest launches such as Sohan Skypark, Sohan Exotica, and Sohan Fortune, we have stood for excellence in construction, transparency in transactions, and lasting customer satisfaction.

Why Sohan Reaalty

Built on Trust & Ethics

Every project by Sohan Reaalty is backed by strong ethical values: fair pricing, clear paperwork, timely delivery, and honest communication. Our growing community of homeowners trusts us because we keep our promises.

Thoughtful Design & Quality Construction

We pay attention to details. Be it vastu compliance, reliable structural engineering, or finishes that last — every apartment and plot is designed for durability, comfort, and aesthetic appeal. Our projects also focus on open spaces, ventilation, and modern amenities.

Strategic Locations

Our developments are in well-connected, high-growth neighborhoods of Bengaluru. Whether it's Hebbal-adjacent Sohan Exotica, Varthur's Sohan Fortune, or the upcoming Sohan Skypark in Jakkur, we choose locations that offer convenience, infrastructure, and appreciation potential.

Range & Flexibility

From 2 & 3 BHK premium flats to plots and gated communities, we offer a variety of housing and investment options. Our portfolio is tailored to meet diverse needs and budgets without compromising on quality.

Customer-First Approach

Our clients are at the center of everything we do. From acquisition to handover, we ensure smooth processes, clear updates, and after-sales support. Our reputation has been built largely through word-of-mouth and repeated trust.

Sustainable & Future-Ready Development

At Sohan Reaalty, we believe great homes should also build a better tomorrow. Our projects integrate eco-conscious practices such as rainwater harvesting, green spaces, energy efficiency, and EV readiness — making our communities not just modern, but future-ready.
