Channel Partner Program

At Sohan Realty, we see our Channel Partners not just as associates, but as trusted collaborators in growth. By joining hands with us, partners gain access to premium projects, transparent processes, and the opportunity to maximize their business potential with one of the most credible names in real estate.

Opportunities for All

We actively collaborate with both established firms and independent freelancers who bring professionalism, integrity, and a strong portfolio. With our support, every partner is empowered to achieve sustainable growth and long-term success.

Comprehensive Training

Guidance on lead registration, processes, and project insights.

Customer Engagement Support

Assistance in client interactions, ensuring seamless experiences.

Transparent Price Management

Clear pricing strategies that build trust and confidence with customers.

Who is our Ideal Channel Partner?

As we focus on cultivating the right relationships, we follow a number of metrics to select our partners:

  • Are they equipped?
  • Do they have RERA Certification & the resources to invest in the partnership?
  • Are they enthusiastic?
  • Will the partnership help them reach their goals?
  • Are they efficient?
  • Is there a technical fit? A competence fit?
  • Do we have similar values?
  • Is there a culture match between both parties?
